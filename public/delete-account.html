Account support

Delete your account

Last updated November 20, 2026

OpenCourts is operated by Big Data Analytica LLC. You can delete your account on this website without installing the app.

Delete through Account

  1. Open Account and sign in using the same Google or Apple account you use for OpenCourts.
  2. If you own active groups, open My Groups and archive them first. Archiving closes the group and cannot be undone in the app.
  3. Return to Account, choose Delete my account and type DELETE.
  4. Choose Permanently delete. If asked to sign in again, sign out and sign back in, then retry; deletion requires a sign-in within the last 15 minutes.

What is removed?

Deletion removes your player profile, sign-in connections and sessions, organized games and related records, and archived groups you own. Other players' accounts and independently organized activities remain. Your ordinary authored chat messages and named chat membership notices are removed. Device push subscriptions and associated delivery records are deleted. Current photo references are removed; stored-image cleanup and external caches can take longer. See the Privacy policy for retention and backup limits.

What may remain?

Reported message snapshots and limited context are kept while a safety case is open and normally for 90 days after resolution. A documented legal or unresolved-appeal preservation hold can retain evidence and held conversation messages longer, including names or text captured in a report. Your account references are removed from retained case records. Other safety and moderation records may remain with account references removed. Recipient copies and hosting-provider backups may persist; immediate erasure from every copy is not promised. Read chat retention and preservation details before deleting.

Cannot sign in or finish deletion?

Email support@opencourts.us to request account deletion. Tell us which sign-in provider you used. We may need to verify ownership before acting. Never send passwords, sign-in codes or tokens. This email request does not immediately delete your account.
