WHAT THE DATA MEANS

Radar narrows the search.

OpenCourts compares estimated precipitation at court locations. It cannot tell you whether the playing surface has dried.

Compare readings

Look at the one-, three-, and six-hour estimates. When complete readings are available, courts with less recent estimated rain appear first.

Check radar quality

The quality label describes whether readings are complete and consistent. It is not a confidence rating that a court is dry.

Account for what radar misses

A new shower, shade, drainage, and court material can change conditions. Forecast snow and ice cautions are shown separately from radar scores.

BEFORE YOU PLAY

Use the estimate, then inspect the court.

01

Start with the lower-rain locations

A lower estimate points you toward courts to check; it does not clear them for play.

02

Look at the surface

Watch for standing water, slippery patches, and shaded areas that remain wet.

03

Respect closures

Check venue notices or posted rules before using a court after rain.

Radar samples precipitation over an area, not at the net. If readings are incomplete, OpenCourts may not show a court ranking.
