FIND A GOOD FIT

Make the search work for your game.

Use a few simple details to make a local search more useful, then decide whether to invite someone.

Start with a city or area

Search by city, general area, or ZIP code. Location matching is intentionally broad; it is not a live distance tracker and does not reveal a street address.

Choose a skill preference

Filter by a self-reported level—beginner, intermediate, advanced, or all levels. Players can update their own profile details at any time.

Use activity as a hint

Approximate recent activity can help you decide who to invite. It is not a schedule, an availability signal, or a promise that someone will reply.

FROM SEARCH TO COURT

Send an invitation people can answer.

01

Make your profile useful

Add a display name, optional general location, and skill preference. Turn on player discovery only if you want other signed-in players to find you.

02

Search the area you can reach

Try a nearby city or ZIP code and adjust the skill preference if the results are limited. Only active players who opted in appear.

03

Organize a public game

Choose a court and time, then invite a player from search. Invitations do not reserve a place; the game’s join and approval rules still apply.

04

Give the invite context

State the format, expected level, and any useful court details in the game description. A clear plan makes it easier for someone to decide.

QUICK ANSWERS

Finding a tennis partner, clearly.

Can I find tennis players without signing in?

No. Player search requires an OpenCourts account and sign-in. Only active players who choose to be discoverable appear in results.

Does player search show an exact location?

No. Search uses a general city or area, not a precise distance or street address. Adding a general location to your profile is optional.

Can I message someone directly from player search?

Player search lets you invite a discoverable player to a public game you organize. It does not show private group activity or expose a player’s email address.

Does “Active today” mean they are free to play?

No. Activity is approximate and does not show a player’s schedule or guarantee a response. Include a clear time and court in your game invitation.

Player discovery is optional and account-only. Search results depend on active players in the area who have opted in. General location and recent activity are provided as matching context, not exact location or availability.
