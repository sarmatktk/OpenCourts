OpenCourts is operated by Big Data Analytica LLC, registered in Virginia, United States. This notice explains how we handle information when you browse courts, check weather, or use Play Together. The Android app displays website content, so these website practices also apply there.

OpenCourts does not sell personal information. Play Together is free to use. You can browse courts without creating an account; an account is needed for community actions such as organizing or joining games.

Accounts and information you provide

Google and Apple handle sign-in. OpenCourts receives an account identifier and the profile information returned by your provider, such as your email address and, when supplied, your name. Apple may supply a private relay email instead of your personal address. We do not receive or store your Google or Apple password.

We store your display name, optional bio, general location, self-reported skill level, player-discovery preference and optional photo. We also store the games and groups you create, membership and role records, invitations, join and guest requests, participation decisions, cancellations, optional cancellation reasons, notifications, blocks and reports you submit. Do not put private contact details, sensitive information or a home address in profile fields or public content.

Connected sign-in email addresses are visible in your own Account settings, not in player-search results or group member lists. To use one account with both providers, connect the second method while signed in. Matching names or email addresses do not automatically merge accounts.

Who can see what

  • Player discovery: listing is optional and off by default. If enabled, signed-in players can search your display name, general location, skill and bio. Turning it off removes you from player discovery; it does not hide your participation or membership from people authorized to see that game or group.
  • Public games: game titles, court locations, start times, organizer display names, notes and place counts are publicly discoverable. Host controls and pending requests have additional access restrictions. Treat information you put in a public game as public.
  • Unlisted games: excluded from public game search, with access through a valid invitation link, a recipient-specific in-app invitation, or existing authorization. Direct player invitations grant access only to the recipient and expire within seven days or at game start. A recipient can forward a link, so unlisted does not mean confidential or group-private. The host can revoke links; requesting a place still requires approval.
  • Public and private groups: both are discoverable. Nonmembers can see the group name, optional public description, general location, visibility label, public cover and a way to request membership. Public-group events, including their schedules and court locations, are visible to everyone. Private-group events require approved membership. Member lists and other members-only content require approved membership in either group type. Nonmembers need host approval to join public-group events; approved members can join directly. Private-group games, schedules, meeting locations, participant lists and membership-management details are not part of the public preview.
  • Organizers: authorized hosts and group managers can access the membership or participation information needed for their roles. Group invitation links do not bypass approval. Removing a member revokes their access to private group content. A link cannot override membership checks.
  • Notifications and reports: activity notifications go to the relevant participants or managers. Hosts receive cancellation information, including an optional reason. Designated site moderators can review submitted report text and references; being a group owner does not make someone a site moderator. Report-review access does not grant general access to private group content. Site moderators can search account display names, IDs and status for safety enforcement and can suspend or restore Play Together accounts. Suspension revokes sign-in sessions and device subscriptions to stop future sends; already-sent messages cannot be recalled. Account and activity records remain. Moderation decisions, reasons, moderator references and timestamps are recorded for review. Decision history can remain after account deletion with account references removed. Contact support@opencourts.us to appeal or request deletion if suspension prevents you from using account controls.

Guest places and waiting players: we store the account bringing the guests, the guest count and its request or confirmation status; guest places do not create player accounts, group membership or chat access. Group owners, co-owners and organizers, and non-group game hosts, can confirm their own guests without separate approval once they have a confirmed playing place. Other players need approval. When an organizer increases capacity, they may choose to confirm waitlisted players and pending join requests automatically: reserved offers are honored first, followed by remaining requests in order. Your participation status, notifications and chat eligibility can change when your place is confirmed.

Recipients can copy or capture information they were allowed to see. Revoking a link or removing a member cannot erase copies already made outside OpenCourts.

Optional phone and browser notifications

Where available, you can opt in from Account to receive push notifications. We store a browser push subscription endpoint, encryption keys, account/session association, category preferences and delivery status. Push services operated by your browser provider carry encrypted messages and may receive delivery metadata such as destination, timing and network information. Lock-screen messages describe the activity type without private names, locations, times or cancellation reasons. Tapping opens the related game or group; sign-in and current access checks still apply.

You can disable this device or change game/group preferences in Account, and manage notification permission in browser or Android settings. Signing out stops future sends for that session, but cannot recall messages already sent to a provider. Your own game and chat actions do not generate activity notifications for you. Chat notification settings can be changed separately for each conversation. Delivery is not guaranteed. The delivery worker removes expired event records after their delivery window (normally 24 hours, shorter for waitlist offers); inbox history follows the separate community retention policy. Account deletion removes associated device subscriptions and delivery records. These are browser-based push notifications, including on compatible iPhone Home Screen apps. Availability depends on your device, browser and installed app version. Email activity notifications are not currently offered.

Location, searches and browser storage

We request device location only when you choose a location action such as Near me or Use my location. Switching to the Map view does not request your location. If permission is already granted, your browser may supply it without another prompt. Coordinates are sent with court, map and weather requests. They are not saved as your player-profile address. You can deny access and search by city, address or ZIP code instead.

Search text and coordinates are processed to find places and courts. Place suggestions and searches may be cached in the current tab. Your last chosen court-search location is saved in browser local storage to restore it on return. Favorites and other device preferences may also use local storage. Clear site data to remove browser-stored information; this does not delete your account or server records. Public court results from the last searched area can be reused on this device for up to 24 hours to show courts while live results load. Saved search data remains on the device until replaced or cleared through site settings. If an update fails, saved results may remain visible with a warning; confirm court details before travelling. Public group cover images can remain in browser or intermediary caches for up to one hour after removal; private profile photos and community account APIs are not publicly cached.

Profile and player-search address suggestions are reduced to a general area; a street address is not a profile-location field. Game court locations are different: a selected court or manually entered facility name, address and map coordinates are stored with the game and shown according to that game's visibility. Never use a private home address for a public game.

Game chat

Game and series conversations contain text messages, author references, timestamps, conversation notification preferences and a last-read position used for unread counts. People with chat access can see the display names and roles of others who currently have access. This is not an online-status or read-receipt feature. New eligible participants can see earlier retained messages.

Standalone game chat is shared with the organizer, confirmed players and valid invitees who still have game access. Private-group membership is also required for private-group games. Leaving or being removed from a standalone game ends its chat access, except for its authorized organizer. Group recurring series use one shared series chat instead of a separate chat for each date. Confirming a place in a series game admits an active group member. Ordinary members retain access if they confirmed a series place within the last 30 days, sent a message in the last 30 days, or still have a confirmed place in an upcoming series game or one that ended within the last 30 days. Otherwise they lose access after both 30-day activity periods have elapsed. Cancelled games do not count as upcoming or recently ended games. Reading and muting do not renew that period. Group owners, co-owners, group organizers and the current series organizer have role-based access without confirming a playing place and are exempt from inactivity expiry while otherwise authorized and actively belonging to the group. Losing a role removes this exemption; ordinary participant rules then apply. Leaving or being removed from the group ends access immediately. Ordinary participants need a new confirmed place to rejoin after removal or expiry; returning to the group alone is insufficient. Current authorized group staff can regain role-based access when active membership is restored. These rules use confirmed participation, not verified physical attendance. Small notices record chat joins, departures and inactivity expiry; they do not generate push notifications.

Standalone game replies close 24 hours after the game ends; cancellation makes that game's chat read-only immediately. Its ordinary messages become inaccessible 30 days after game end or cancellation. Each series message and membership notice becomes inaccessible 30 days after it was posted, even if the series continues. Series messages can continue while the series and group remain eligible and the conversation has not expired; canceling one date does not close other dates' shared conversation. Active groups retain series conversation metadata, including read and notification settings, so authorized group staff can return after quiet periods. Individual messages still expire after 30 days. Archiving or deleting the group ends access; canceled conversations may be read-only. Scheduled cleanup deletes expired ordinary messages and conversations; deletion may finish after the access cutoff. Safety evidence and preservation holds have separate rules below. You can mute one conversation while keeping unread inbox updates. Chat pushes use generic wording without message text. Blocking hides messages between the two accounts and prevents access to each other's hosted chat; it does not erase copies already seen.

Chat uses HTTPS in transit and Cloudflare's platform encryption at rest. It is not end-to-end encrypted: OpenCourts processes message text to provide the service and handle safety reports. We do not send chat text to Google Analytics or put it in public search listings. Do not post passwords, sensitive information or private contact details.

Reporting a message sends designated site moderators the report explanation, the reported message and up to four nearby visible messages as context. This evidence is stored separately from ordinary chat. It remains while a case is open and is normally deleted 90 days after the case is resolved. Moderators can hide a reported message and take account action. A documented preservation hold for an unresolved appeal or legal obligation can retain the remaining conversation and case evidence beyond the ordinary deadlines. Holds have a 30-day review date and must be explicitly released when no longer necessary; a review date does not automatically destroy held evidence. Moderator evidence actions and reasons are recorded. Ordinary expired chats remain unavailable to participants during a hold.

Account deletion removes your messages from ordinary conversations and removes your account references from retained case records. Messages in a held conversation and report snapshots may remain for safety handling or required preservation, including names or text captured in the report. Deleting an organizer's account may remove associated games and ordinary game chats. Shared series conversations follow current group and series access rules; your ordinary authored messages and named membership notices are removed. Held evidence can remain. Backups and recovery copies can persist under the hosting provider's recovery windows, and copies made by recipients cannot be recalled. Contact support@opencourts.us about a particular record or preservation request.

Photos

You can upload one player photo and one cover per group. Player photos are currently available only to the account holder, including their own header avatar. Group covers are public, even for private groups. Only the owner and co-owners can change a group cover.

Images are resized and re-encoded before storage, with embedded image metadata removed. Cropping lets you choose the visible area of your avatar. Removing or replacing a photo removes its current reference; storage cleanup can be delayed. Metadata removal does not remove identifying details visible in the picture itself.

Service providers and external links

Cloudflare hosts the website and its server functions, stores community records in D1 and photos in R2, and processes network requests. Hosting and security services receive technical information such as IP addresses, request details, browser information and timestamps.

Google and Apple process authentication under their own privacy notices. OpenStreetMap supplies map tiles and court data; Photon and Nominatim support place suggestions and searches; Overpass providers supply map details. Weather sources include the U.S. National Weather Service, NOAA radar, MET Norway, Open-Meteo and WeatherAPI, depending on coverage and availability. These services receive the text, coordinates or network requests needed to respond. Map tiles and online fonts can be requested directly by your browser.

Directions open Google Maps with the court destination. Optional support contributions open Buy Me a Coffee. Those services handle their own interactions and any payments; OpenCourts does not process Play Together subscriptions or store payment-card details.

Analytics and cookies

OpenCourts pages, including Account and Play Together, load Google Analytics to measure page usage. We send a generic page label and an approved page path without query strings or fragments, suppress the referrer, and do not send names, email addresses, game or group identifiers, invitation tokens, search text, chat text or report contents in our analytics events. Unknown page paths are recorded as a generic not-found page. Automatic enhanced measurement is disabled; opening chat or changing a page fragment does not create an additional page-view event. Google signals and advertising personalization signals are disabled in our tag configuration. Analytics may receive device/browser information and approximate location, and may set first-party cookies such as _ga. Invitation links may still appear in your browser history or other services' records. See Google's privacy notice.

Essential session and temporary sign-in cookies support authentication and request protection. Sessions normally expire after seven days; you can sign out sooner or sign out on all devices from Account. Browser cookie restrictions may prevent sign-in. Browser privacy settings and content blockers can restrict analytics; this site does not currently provide a separate analytics-consent toggle.

Security

We use HTTPS, server-side access checks, protected session cookies and request validation. Session tokens are stored as hashes, and retained Apple refresh tokens are encrypted for token-revocation actions. These measures reduce risk but do not guarantee absolute security. Keep invitation links and your sign-in accounts secure.

Your choices and account deletion

In Account, you can edit your profile, change discovery visibility, remove your photo, connect a sign-in method, sign out or request account deletion. You can also leave groups, cancel participation and block or report players through the relevant screens. Blocking does not remove existing shared memberships or confirmed games.

Account deletion requires a sign-in within the last 15 minutes and typing DELETE to confirm. If asked to sign in again, sign out and sign back in using the same provider, then retry deletion. First archive any active groups you own. Archiving closes a group to new members and removes access to its member list and private details; it cannot be undone in the app. Deletion removes your profile and linked sign-in/session records, your organized games and related records through their account relationships; archived groups you own are also deleted. Other users' accounts and independently organized activities are not deleted. Apple-linked deletion attempts to revoke the stored Apple token; if that cannot complete, the site reports a failure rather than claiming deletion succeeded.

For step-by-step instructions, see Delete your account. For access, correction, deletion or other privacy questions that the interface cannot resolve, email the contact below. We may need to verify account ownership. A self-service account export is not currently provided. Do not email passwords, private keys or sign-in tokens.

Data retention

Community records are retained to provide accounts, game history, memberships, notifications and safety handling until removed through available controls or account deletion. Invitations expire or move to History without necessarily being deleted. Notification or directory display limits are not deletion schedules. Expired sign-in attempts and expired or revoked session records are cleaned during later sign-in activity.

Apart from the game-chat and message-report schedules above, there is not currently a fixed automatic deletion schedule for all inactive accounts, historical activities, other reports or uploaded objects awaiting cleanup. Hosting, analytics, email and support-service copies follow their respective retention practices; immediate erasure from every backup or external copy is not promised. Contact us about a specific record.

Age requirements and changes to this notice

Public court browsing is available without an account. Play Together accounts are for adults 18 and older. We ask for an adult-age confirmation before sign-in; we do not collect a date of birth or verify identity through this confirmation. Guest places must not be used to bypass the adult-age requirement. Play Together is not designed for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information that should be removed, contact us. We will update this notice as features or data practices change; the date above identifies the latest revision.
