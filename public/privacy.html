OpenCourts policies

Privacy

Last updated October 9, 2026

OpenCourts is a free court discovery service. We do not sell personal information.

Location

Your browser location is requested only when you choose a location button. Coordinates are used to load nearby court and weather data. A selected search location may be stored in your browser’s local storage for convenience. You can remove it by clearing site data.

External services

When you submit a place search, the search text is sent to the OpenStreetMap Nominatim geocoder. Court searches are served through Cloudflare Pages Functions and the Overpass API. Forecasts come from the U.S. National Weather Service. These services receive the query or approximate coordinates needed to fulfill your request and have their own privacy practices.

Questions? Email hello@opencourts.us.
