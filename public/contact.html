Get in touch

Contact

For OpenCourts questions, privacy requests, account trouble, court-data corrections or feedback, email support@opencourts.us.

Report a problem

Include the page URL, device/browser, the steps you took and what you expected. Screenshots help, but remove personal information, invitation tokens and account details. Never send passwords, OAuth secrets, private keys or session cookies.

Privacy and accounts

Use Account for profile changes, discovery preferences, sign-out and deletion. Archive owned groups before deleting your account. If a control fails, explain the issue by email; we may need to verify ownership. Read the privacy notice for what deletion covers.

Safety reports

Use the in-site Report action for a player, game, group or chat message, and explain what happened. Message reports include the message and limited nearby context for site moderators. Designated moderators review submitted reports; reports are not an emergency channel and we do not promise immediate review. For an immediate danger, contact local emergency services. Group membership decisions can also be raised with the group's owner or co-owners.

Court information

Court records can be corrected on OpenStreetMap. For court hours, access, availability or reservations, contact the venue directly. OpenCourts does not operate the courts.
