About OpenCourts

Find your court. Play your game.

OpenCourts helps people discover public tennis courts, check lighting details, and plan around local weather. The service is free and does not offer reservations or payments.

Open data, with care

Court locations come from OpenStreetMap contributors. Coverage and details vary by area. Court access, lights, hours, and amenities can change, so confirm locally before you travel.

Weather is a guide

Hourly forecasts come from the U.S. National Weather Service. Playability estimates summarize forecast and recent-rain data; they are not a safety or availability guarantee.
