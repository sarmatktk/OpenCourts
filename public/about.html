About OpenCourts

Find a court. Find your tennis community.

OpenCourts helps tennis players find courts, plan around the weather and organize games. Court discovery is available without an account. Play Together adds player profiles, games, groups and invitations to OpenCourts. Use the website for the latest Play Together features; installed-app features depend on the app version.

Play Together

Sign in with Google or Apple to create a player profile, discover public games, request a place, invite players and manage your schedule. Groups can organize member-only games. Both public and private groups are discoverable. Public-group events can be viewed by everyone; private-group events and all group member lists require approved membership.

Signed-in players start with a Feed of their groups and upcoming activities. Hosts approve public-game requests, manage guest places and notify players through the site's inbox. Unlisted games use shareable invitation links. Public-group events are publicly visible; private-group events require approved membership even if someone forwards the URL.

Play Together is free to use. Weekly series are supported, with separate attendance for each date. Creating a game does not book a court, guarantee access or confirm another person's identity or skill. Read help and safety information and our privacy notice.

Play Together accounts are for adults 18 and older. Game chat helps authorized participants coordinate; group weekly series share a conversation across dates, with 30-day message retention. Optional phone and browser notifications depend on device permission and your settings. Public court browsing stays available without an account.

Find and compare nearby tennis courts

Browse public tennis courts on a map or in a list. Sort by distance or listed lighting, and filter by distance, hard, clay or grass surface, and confirmed public access. Court listings may include the number of courts, surface, lighting, address, nearby place names, and directions when that information is available.

Check recent precipitation near courts

Compare nearby courts using recent NOAA radar precipitation estimates. OpenCourts shows one-, three-, and six-hour readings, a radar quality label, and courts with less recent precipitation. These are area estimates, not measurements of whether a court surface is dry.

Plan with a seven-day playing forecast

See available National Weather Service forecast periods between estimated local sunrise and sunset. The play score considers temperature, wind, and rain probability. Low-scoring stretches stay visible with labels for conditions such as rain, snow, ice, heat, wind, or cold. The planner also shows heat index when available and local weather alerts. Forecast conditions are for the surrounding area and cannot confirm whether a particular court is playable.

Where the information comes from

The map and court listings use OpenStreetMap data, maintained by contributors. Some courts may be missing, and details such as access, lights, or amenities can be out of date. Check with the venue before you go.

Forecasts use the U.S. National Weather Service where supported, with MET Norway, Open-Meteo or WeatherAPI depending on coverage and availability. Recent-rain estimates use NOAA radar data. They can help with planning, but they do not measure the court surface or guarantee that a court is open or playable.
