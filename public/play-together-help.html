Play Together

Help and safety

Last updated November 18, 2026

Play Together is being tested in Preview. Use the stable Preview link, rather than a numbered deployment URL, for sign-in and sharing. Test data may be reset. The existing live court website and native iOS app have not been replaced.

Accounts and navigation

Use Google or Apple to sign in. To connect both to the same OpenCourts account, add the second method in Account while signed in; identical names and emails do not automatically merge accounts. Your provider controls password recovery. Feed shows your groups and upcoming games; Find Games discovers public games; My Games shows your schedule and requests. Courts returns to the main court website. Notifications and Account are available in the header.

Create a game

Choose an existing court, or enter a facility name and address if lookup fails or your court is missing. Locate and confirm the address before continuing. Manual locations are organizer-provided and do not create public catalog records. Choose a future start in the court's time zone, duration, singles/doubles format, courts planned, total player capacity, skill preference and notes. Two singles courts suggest four players; four doubles courts suggest sixteen. Capacity includes the host and approved guests and can be increased for rotating players, up to 128. Courts planned is not a reservation. The host occupies one place. Confirm access and remember: this is an invitation to play, not a venue booking.

Group game waitlists

When a group game is full, approved members can join its waitlist. A released place is offered in signup order and reserved for that player for up to two hours, ending sooner at game start. In My Games, choose Waitlisted to see your position or offer deadline, accept a place or leave the queue. These actions are also available in game details. Hosts can see the queue and active offers in game management. Offers and expired-offer advancement update when the game or inbox refreshes; notifications are in-app, not email or push. Each offer is for one player; guests still need host approval and available capacity. Each weekly occurrence has a separate waitlist.

Weekly series

Choose Repeat weekly and 2-12 weeks total to schedule the same weekday and local time. Each occurrence is a separate game with its own invitations, requests, guests and attendance. Joining one week does not join other weeks. Hosts can open Manage series from game details to reschedule or cancel This game only or This and future games. Review affected dates before confirming; time changes can preserve individually rescheduled exceptions. Past games stay unchanged. Each date retains its own attendance, and affected players receive in-app updates. All dates must be within 180 days; a series with an invalid clock-change time must be rescheduled before creation.

Choose the right audience

  • Public: discoverable by players; join requests require host approval.
  • Unlisted: absent from public search; a valid shared link grants its intended access, but joining still requires host approval. Links can be forwarded and revoked.
  • Group events: public-group events are visible to everyone; nonmembers request host approval to join. Private-group events are visible only to approved members. Approved members can join directly when space is available. Forwarding a private-group game link does not grant access.

Invitations, requests and guests

Player search lists people who choose to be discoverable. Use Invite players inside a public or unlisted game you host, or invite from player search to a public game; the game allows up to ten distinct invitees total. Repeated sends do not count twice. Invitations reserve no places and do not bypass approval. When a game starts, unanswered invitations expire; completed and cancelled invitations appear in History.

A pending request does not occupy a place. Players can withdraw pending requests or cancel an approved place, optionally explaining why to the host. Request guest places through the game controls; guests count toward capacity only when approved. Guest approval does not create an account or private-group membership for a guest. Do not share restricted group content with a guest.

Groups and roles

Public and private groups are both searchable. Public-group events appear in public discovery and the All feed, including for nonmembers. Private-group previews are discoverable, but their events are members-only. Member lists and other members-only content stay restricted in both group types. Group invitations lead to the approval workflow. Owners and co-owners manage the group. Organizers create and manage their own group events. The permanent primary Owner assigns roles and archives the group. A group can have at most one Co-owner and one Organizer. Legacy Admin roles may retain existing permissions until updated.

If an organizer leaves the group or loses organizer permission, upcoming group games transfer to the primary owner. Removing a member revokes private content access. Owners must archive their active groups before deleting their accounts.

Notifications and safety

Check the bell for in-site requests, decisions and cancellation updates. These are not a guarantee of email or device push delivery. Blocking hides players from each other's discovery and prevents new joining requests to each other's hosted games, but does not remove existing shared memberships or confirmed places. Use Report for harassment, spam, unsafe content or privacy concerns. Designated moderators review reports; group roles do not automatically grant site moderation.

Be respectful, get permission before posting another person's photo or information, and do not post private contact details. Skill preferences are self-reported, not verified ratings. OpenCourts does not verify every participant's identity or guarantee their conduct. Confirm plans with the host, use appropriate public venues, follow venue rules and check weather and actual court conditions.

Test feedback and help

Try phone and desktop layouts, light/dark mode, location permission, sign-in, requests and group privacy using separate host/player accounts. Report the device/browser, URL, steps and result through Contact. Redact private information. Recurring games, native iOS integration and paid plans are not part of the current test release.
