Game conversations

Use Game chat on a game page to coordinate with its organizer, confirmed players and valid invitees. Current access rules apply, including private-group membership. Group recurring series share one Series chat across their dates; standalone games keep their own Game chat. Confirming a series place admits an active group member. Ordinary participants stay if they confirmed a series place in the last 30 days, sent a message in the last 30 days, or still have a confirmed place in an upcoming series game or one that ended in the last 30 days. They lose access only when both activity periods have elapsed and no such confirmed place remains. Cancelled games do not count as upcoming or recently ended games. Reading alone does not extend access. Leaving the group ends access immediately; ordinary participants need a new confirmed place to rejoin after removal or expiry. Group owners, co-owners, group organizers and the current series organizer keep role-based access without playing or posting while otherwise authorized. Active group membership is required; current staff can regain role-based access when that membership is restored. Losing the role removes this exemption. Quiet notices show who was added, left or was removed after inactivity without sending push alerts. Removing a player from a standalone game ends their chat access immediately; its authorized organizer keeps access when only releasing their own playing place.

Messages are shared with all eligible participants, including people who join later. Standalone game replies close 24 hours after game end, or immediately on cancellation; those messages expire 30 days after game end or cancellation. Each series message expires 30 days after posting. Canceling one date does not close the shared series chat. Active groups retain series conversation metadata, including read and notification settings, so authorized group staff can return after quiet periods. Individual messages still expire after 30 days. Archiving or deleting the group ends access; canceled conversations may be read-only.

Chat is text only and is not end-to-end encrypted. Keep it respectful and avoid private contact details. Report a message with an explanation for site moderator review; personal blocks hide messages between the two accounts. Mute chat push to stop this conversation's message pushes without losing unread inbox updates.

Reported messages and limited context are kept while a case is open and normally for 90 days after resolution. Documented legal or unresolved-appeal preservation holds can extend retention. See game-chat privacy and retention for access, account deletion, moderator access and backup details.

Accounts and navigation

Play Together accounts are for adults 18 and older. Confirm your age, then use Google or Apple to sign in. To connect both to the same OpenCourts account, add the second method in Account while signed in; identical names and emails do not automatically merge accounts. Your provider controls password recovery. Feed shows your groups and upcoming games; Find Games discovers public games; My Games shows your schedule and requests. Courts returns to the main court website. Notifications and Account are available in the header.

Create a game

Choose an existing court, or enter a facility name and address if lookup fails or your court is missing. Locate and confirm the address before continuing. Manual locations are organizer-provided and do not create public catalog records. Choose a future start in the court's time zone, duration, singles/doubles format, courts planned, total player capacity, skill preference and notes. Two singles courts suggest four players; four doubles courts suggest sixteen. Capacity includes the host and approved guests and can be increased for rotating players, up to 128. Courts planned is not a reservation. The host initially occupies one place. Organizers can release their own playing place while keeping group and non-group games running and retaining management access. Rejoining requires an available place and respects group waitlist reservations. This changes only the selected occurrence of a weekly series. Cancel game remains a separate action for cancelling the game for everyone. Confirm access and remember: this is an invitation to play, not a venue booking.

Organizers can edit the game format, courts planned and total player capacity in game management. Increasing capacity offers a choice to confirm waiting players automatically: existing reserved offers are honored first, then waitlisted players and pending join requests are combined in request order. Confirmed players and approved guests keep their places. Increasing capacity does not automatically approve guest requests. Group owners, co-owners and organizers, and hosts of non-group games, can add their own guests directly when they have a confirmed playing place and space is available. Other players still need guest approval. Settings apply only to the selected date of a series.

Group game waitlists

When a group game is full, approved members can join its waitlist. A released place is offered in signup order and reserved for that player for up to two hours, ending sooner at game start. In My Games, choose Waitlisted to see your position or offer deadline, accept a place or leave the queue. These actions are also available in game details. Hosts can see the queue and active offers in game management. Offers update when game activity refreshes and through the notification delivery worker where enabled. Updates appear in the in-app inbox; optional push delivery depends on your device and notification preferences. Each offer is for one player; ordinary players' guests still need host approval; all guests require available capacity. Each weekly occurrence has a separate waitlist.

Weekly series

Choose Repeat weekly and 2-12 weeks total to schedule the same weekday and local time. Each occurrence is a separate game with its own invitations, requests, guests and attendance. Joining one week does not join other weeks. Hosts can open Manage series from game details to reschedule or cancel This game only or This and future games. Review affected dates before confirming; time changes can preserve individually rescheduled exceptions. Past games stay unchanged. Each date retains its own attendance, and affected players receive in-app updates. All dates must be within 180 days; a series with an invalid clock-change time must be rescheduled before creation.

Choose the right audience

  • Public: discoverable by players; join requests require host approval.
  • Unlisted: absent from public search; a valid shared link grants its intended access, but joining still requires host approval. Links can be forwarded and revoked.
  • Group events: public-group events are visible to everyone; nonmembers request host approval to join. Private-group events are visible only to approved members. Approved members can join directly when space is available. Forwarding a private-group game link does not grant access.

Invitations, requests and guests

Player search lists people who choose to be discoverable. Use Invite players inside a public or unlisted game you host, or invite from player search to a public game; the game allows up to ten distinct invitees total. Repeated sends do not count twice. Invitations reserve no places and do not bypass approval. When a game starts, unanswered invitations expire; completed and cancelled invitations appear in History.

A pending request does not occupy a place. Players can withdraw pending requests or cancel an approved place, optionally explaining why to the host. Add or request guest places through the game controls. Group owners, co-owners and organizers, and non-group game hosts, can confirm their own guests directly without approval. You must first confirm your own playing place. Other players need approval. Guests count toward capacity, including waitlist reservations, and each player can bring up to three guests. Guest approval does not create an account or private-group membership for a guest. Do not share restricted group content with a guest.

Groups and roles

Public and private groups are both searchable. Public-group events appear in public discovery and the All feed, including for nonmembers. Private-group previews are discoverable, but their events are members-only. Member lists and other members-only content stay restricted in both group types. Group invitations lead to the approval workflow. Owners and co-owners manage the group. Organizers create and manage their own group events. The permanent primary Owner assigns roles and archives the group. A group can have at most one Co-owner and one Organizer. Legacy Admin roles may retain existing permissions until updated.

If an organizer leaves the group or loses organizer permission, upcoming group games transfer to the primary owner. Removing a member revokes private content access. Owners must archive their active groups before deleting their accounts. Archiving closes a group and cannot be undone in the app. To delete your account, open Account and type DELETE. You must have signed in within the last 15 minutes; if asked, sign out and sign back in using the same provider, then retry.

Notifications and safety

Check the bell for in-site requests, decisions and cancellation updates. These are not a guarantee of email or device push delivery. Blocking hides players from each other's discovery and prevents new joining requests to each other's hosted games, but does not remove existing shared memberships or confirmed places. Use Report for harassment, spam, unsafe content or privacy concerns and explain what happened. Authorized site moderators review the shared queue near the top of Players. A Site moderation link appears near the top of community/account pages for moderators. Moderators can suspend or restore account access with a recorded reason; personal blocking is separate. Suspended players can contact support@opencourts.us to appeal. Designated moderators review reports; group roles do not automatically grant site moderation.

Be respectful, get permission before posting another person's photo or information, and do not post private contact details. Skill preferences are self-reported, not verified ratings. OpenCourts does not verify every participant's identity or guarantee their conduct. Confirm plans with the host, use appropriate public venues, follow venue rules and check weather and actual court conditions.

Phone and browser notifications

Where push notifications are available, open Account and choose Enable on this device. Permission is optional; the in-app inbox always works. You can disable a device or change game/group categories there. Sign in and enable again after starting a new session. Notification availability depends on your device and app version. Email notifications are not currently offered.

Help and feedback

If you encounter a problem, report the device/browser, URL, steps and result through Contact. Redact private information. Weekly series are supported, with separate participation for each date. Manage one date or future dates from Manage series.

Member-only group discussions

Use the Discussions tab inside your group to start text posts, reply and like posts or comments. Only active members can read or participate, including in public groups. Discussion posts remain until deleted rather than expiring after 30 days. Authors can edit or delete their own content. Group owners and co-owners can remove content and lock discussions. Deleting a post also deletes its replies and likes. Use the three-dot More options menu on a post, comment, chat message, game, group or player to find Report and explain your concern. Site moderators can review reports and remove reported content. Be respectful, do not spam or share another person's private information. Reply notifications are sent to the author and participants, excluding the sender; likes do not send notifications. You can mute reply notifications for each discussion. See discussion privacy and retention.

Group discussion updates

The Discussions tab shows the number of posts you can view. Owner, Co-owner and Organizer labels identify group staff. Use the three-dot menu on someone else's post or comment to report it; your own content offers editing or deletion instead. New staff posts notify other active members through the existing Group notifications preference in Account. Device permission and push opt-in are still required. Your own posts never notify you.

Profile photos beside names

Chats and discussions show a small avatar beside each name. Initials appear when a photo is unavailable or private. In Account, enable Show my photo beside my name in games and groups to share your uploaded photo with authorized game participants and group members. This is optional and off by default; you can turn it off at any time.
