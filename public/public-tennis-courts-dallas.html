QUICK ANSWERS

Before you grab your racquet.

Are public tennis courts in Dallas free?

Not necessarily. Dallas has ordinary park courts as well as full-service public tennis centers. Public centers may require reservations and court fees, so check the official facility page.

Which Dallas public tennis centers have lights?

Dallas says its four full-service public tennis centers feature lighted courts. Individual city pages confirm lights at Samuell Grand, Fretz, and L.B. Houston.

Can I reserve a Dallas tennis court?

The featured public tennis centers accept reservations under their posted rules. Policies, hours, and fees can change, so confirm directly with the center.
