QUICK ANSWERS

Before you grab your racquet.

Are City of Miami tennis courts free?

Do not assume every court is free. The city says fees and rules for many tennis courts are posted onsite, while permits and reserved use may have separate charges.

Does this include Miami Beach?

No. Miami Beach and Miami-Dade County operate separate park systems. This guide focuses only on parks within the City of Miami.

How should I plan around Miami weather?

Check hourly conditions, heat guidance, recent rainfall, and active weather alerts before leaving. Conditions can vary by neighborhood.
