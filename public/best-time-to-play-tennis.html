READ THE FORECAST

A play score with context.

The number helps you compare times. The conditions beside it explain the tradeoffs.

Rain chance

A low chance makes a small difference; a high chance lowers the score much more. Look at the percentage shown for the actual playing window.

Temperature and wind

Temperatures from 60°F to 78°F have no temperature penalty. Wind up to 8 mph has no wind penalty. When humidity data is available, hot-weather scoring uses the estimated heat index.

Winter conditions

Forecast snow or ice caps the play score at 20/100. The caution can continue after the forecast event, so check the court surface before playing.
