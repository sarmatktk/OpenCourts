QUICK ANSWERS

Planning and scheduling tennis games.

How do I organize a tennis game?

Choose a date and start time, pick a court, set the format and player capacity, then share the game or invite players. You can manage join requests from your account.

Can I schedule a weekly tennis game?

Yes. Game creation supports a weekly series with multiple dates. Check the dates, court, and details before sharing it with players.

Does creating a game reserve a court?

No. A game listing helps organize players and details; it does not book a public court. Check the facility's rules and make any reservation directly with the court operator.

Can I approve players before they join?

Public games can receive join requests for the organizer to review. You can approve players based on the game’s available spots and your plans.

What should I put in the game details?

Include the date and start time, court, singles or doubles format, expected level, player spots, and useful notes such as fees, equipment, or reservation details.

OpenCourts helps players organize games and coordinate who is playing. A game listing does not reserve a court; confirm availability and booking requirements with the facility.
