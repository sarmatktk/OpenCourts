QUICK ANSWERS

Before you grab your racquet.

Are public tennis courts in Los Angeles free?

Some are. The city distinguishes free, first-come open-play courts from pay-tennis facilities. Confirm the facility type on the official city directory before visiting.

Can I reserve an LA tennis court?

City pay-tennis facilities accept reservations under posted rules and fees. Open-play courts generally do not require a standard paid reservation, though permits may apply for organized use.

Does OpenCourts show whether courts have lights?

Yes, when lighting has been mapped. A missing or unknown label means the data is incomplete, so check the official facility page for confirmation.

OpenCourts court details come from OpenStreetMap contributors and can be incomplete. City hours, fees, closures, and reservation policies can change. Confirm official Los Angeles Recreation and Parks information before you travel. This guide covers the City of Los Angeles, not every neighboring municipality in Los Angeles County.
